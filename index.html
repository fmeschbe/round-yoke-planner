How the calculation works
  • Increase points are evenly spaced; each adds two stitches.
  • Within the increase section, increase every IP / 2 rounds, starting at its round IP / 2. There are IP / 2 − 1 plain rounds between increases.
  • The increase section ends on its final increase round. Optional plain rounds are separate.
  • Use positive whole numbers, even IP, and SI divisible by IP. SF − SI must be divisible by 2 × IP.
  • The preview is schematic, not a model of gauge, stitch ancestry, or tension.
  • When resizing, colors keep their real-stitch position within each section. Changes that remove colored stitches require confirmation.
  • Initial and final plain rounds use SI and SF stitches. They do not change the increase schedule.
